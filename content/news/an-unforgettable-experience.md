Travis with Crystal River Manatee Fun provided us with an unforgettable experience swimming with the manatees today. From start to finish, our adventure with Travis was nothing short of exceptional. He was not only knowledgeable about the manatees and their habitat but also passionate about preserving and protecting these gentle creatures.

From the moment we arrived at the dock, we felt well taken care of. The equipment provided was top-notch, ensuring our comfort and safety during the entire excursion. The thorough briefing before entering the water gave us a clear understanding of how to interact responsibly with the manatees, ensuring a positive experience for both us and the animals.

The highlight of our day was undoubtedly the time spent in the water with the manatees. Thanks to the expertise of Travis, we were able to observe these magnificent creatures up close, without causing any disturbance to their natural behavior. The joy and awe we experienced as we swam alongside and above these gentle giants is indescribable. It was a truly magical encounter that will forever be etched in our memories.

Our experience with Crystal River Manatee Fun was extraordinary. We wholeheartedly recommend them to anyone seeking a remarkable wildlife encounter.

**— Andria DeMarc & Emma Mallon**
